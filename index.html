How it works

Governed underneath. Simple where people work.

MyAi manages the governed capability layer. Ai.S provides a workspace-first environment. S.Ai provides a conversation-first environment. The deeper architecture can include providers, identity and access, credentials, policies, routing, tools, RAG, Studio and self-hosted services.

That structure stays underneath the experience. The person remains at the centre of the work.
