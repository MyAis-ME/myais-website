01 / Why MyAiS exists

It began with a very human frustration.

While developing another technology project in 2026, our founder kept moving between different Ai applications, copying answers and repeating context just to compare their perspectives. He began building Ai.S to solve that problem. One question led to another, and Ai.S grew into MyAiS.

07 / Built and working

Real software. A working ecosystem.

MyAiS is an existing UAE-registered technology company, established in Sharjah in June 2026. The ecosystem is not a concept awaiting its first build. Applications and self-hosted services already exist and operate in the founder's development environment.

Built and operational

  • Ai.S: existing desktop multi-Ai collaboration, conversations and work tools. The latest MMI and WorkConv integration is a continuation of this working application.
  • S.Ai: working browser-based multi-Ai conversations, including provider-specific responses and transparent system status.
  • S.Ai Mobile: existing Android multi-Ai application with direct online provider connections.
  • MyAi Mobile: standalone on-device conversations and Word document creation demonstrated in airplane mode; local image generation also demonstrated.
  • MMI and six MyAi self-hosted role services: developed and running in the founder's environment — management, chat, listening, speech, generation, indexing and knowledge.
  • WorkConv: shared work structure implemented, with S.Ai cloud synchronization demonstrated.

The next stage

  • Complete the latest Ai.S integration with MMI and WorkConv.
  • Extend and validate continuity across the connected applications and supported cloud storage.
  • Advance the additional suite experiences and specialised services, including Ai.G, Ai.S Mobile / Tablet, MyAi Calls and automation.
  • Prepare repeatable customer installations, deployment infrastructure, support and commercial release.
